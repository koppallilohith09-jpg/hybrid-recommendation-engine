How to read the scores

Content scores compare product text with the customer’s interests. Collaborative scores use positively similar rating profiles. Neither score is a purchase probability.

New customers receive a popularity fallback until they leave feedback.

Fictional catalog and seeded demo customers. This workbench is for experimentation, not production customer profiling.

Offline evaluation

Check the blend against held-out positive interactions.

Method

One positive item per eligible customer is removed before ranking. Hit rate measures whether that item appears in the top five. The evaluation always uses a fixed 50/50 blend; the interactive slider does not change this benchmark.

Synthetic behavior does not establish recommendation quality for real customers. No revenue lift or conversion estimate is claimed.
